A shared kit carries them (`films/src/kit.ts` the spec, `films/src/kitScene.tsx` the scene, `pipeline/films/checks/kit.ts` the check): a film is its data module (every number from the engine), a one-line scene file, a project/meta pair, and a check module that builds a `Plan` from the engine alone. The manifest records what the drawn nodes show (every revealed sample, bar height, tip, guide, stage frame, named key); the check recomputes them and carries tampered copies that must fail. Q16/Q17/f1 keep their hand-written scenes. Frame hashes of two full renders of all ten films are identical.

Decisions: Q19 draws even steps only (an odd step moves the walker between centre and rim and leaves every spoke chance unchanged: tested).
