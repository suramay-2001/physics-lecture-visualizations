# Card: `physics/qc` (multi-qubit engine) and its base

`app/src/physics/`: `complex.ts` (`C = {re, im}`; `c, add, sub, mul, scale, conj, abs, abs2, arg, expi, polar, csqrt, cpow, I, ZERO, ONE`), `linalg.ts`
(`Vec = C[]`, `Mat = C[][]`; `inner, norm, normalize, apply, matmul, dagger, outer, identity, isUnitary, isHermitian, matEq`), and `qc/*`. Shared by 448 and 709
(chunk rule (h): never in the entry closure). Qubit order: q0 = leftmost tensor factor = top wire = most significant bit; a basis string `'101'` has q0 = 1.
Every function has a vitest file beside it (`qc/<name>.test.ts`) and, for chapters, a numpy twin in `pipeline/make_qc_fixtures.py` or `pipeline/make_<topic>_fixtures.py`
(`__fixtures__/qc*.json`). `qc/testkit.ts` holds shared test helpers. Limits: `MAX_QUBITS` = 10 and `MAX_DENSE_QUBITS` = 6 (state.ts), `MAX_DENSITY_QUBITS` = 5, circuit `CIRCUIT_LIMITS`.

Pattern for a chapter number: call the engine in `Q.values.ts` (`const circ = simonCircuit(6, 3); V.q18SimonOutChance = marginal(runCircuit(circ, {outcomes:'01'}).states.at(-1)!, [3,4])[1]`),
never recompute it by a second formula in the same file; the numpy twin recomputes it independently. Stage states take the `Circuit` object itself (`{kind:'circuit', circuit, upTo}`).
Gotchas: angles are radians in circuits and `P/Rx/Ry/Rz`; `runCircuit` without `outcomes` refuses mid-circuit measurements; `bell('Phi+')` and `'00+11'` are the same state;
dense routines stop at the qubit caps above (check the constant before planning a bigger picture).
Listing of every export: `docs/context/generated/ids.json` -> `engine`.

**Townsend pass, G3 (448 L9-L10; numpy block `townsendG3` of `make_qc_fixtures.py`, test `qc/townsendG3.test.ts`):** `measure.ts localBasisAmplitudes(psi, ['x'|'y'|'z', ...])` (the amplitudes whose squares are `localBasisProbs`; bar k = overlap with the product ket, bit 0 = the first eigenstate), `entangle.ts singletJoint(thetaA, thetaB)` (P(+a;+b) of the singlet, axes in the x-z plane at polar angles in radians; = 1/2 sin^2((a-b)/2)), `bellBisectGap(Theta)` (P(+a;+b) - P(+a;+c) - P(+c;+b), c halfway; 1/8 at 60 degrees, 0 at 90), `differChance(angleDeg)` (cos^2 of half the angle, from singletJoint), `bits.ts threeAxisTables()` (the eight instruction tables of three axes with the share of ordered axis pairs whose results differ: 1 for two tables, 1/3 for six). Claim twins of the 70 new 448 keys: `pipeline/claims_448/g3.py` -> `__fixtures__/claims-448/g3.json` (merged by glob in `claims.test.ts`).

**Townsend pass, G2a (448 L5-L6; numpy `pipeline/claims_448/g2a.py` -> `__fixtures__/claims-448/g2a.json`, 55 keys + `cases.portProbs`; test `physics/townsendG2a.test.ts`):** `spin.ts portProbs(psi, phiDeg)` -> {plus, minus}, the two ports of a spin interferometer whose one arm is turned by R_z(phi) (phi in DEGREES; ports receive (I +- R_z)psi/2, so for |+x> the + port is (1 + cos(phi/2))/2 with period 720 deg). Every other L5-L6 number reuses `spin.ts` (`nDotSigma`, `eigenHermitian2`, `rotation`, `operatorInBasis`, `ketAlong`, `blochAngles`).
**Townsend pass, G4a (448 L11-L12; numpy script `pipeline/claims_448/g4a.py` -> `__fixtures__/claims-448/g4a.json` for the 51 claim keys and `__fixtures__/dynamics-g4a.json` for the engine tables; test `physics/dynamicsG4a.test.ts`):** `dynamics.ts` `ammoniaH(E0, A)`, `ammoniaPeriod(A)` (= pi/A), `ammoniaChances(E0, A, t)` ({above, below} from the evolved |1>), `energySpread(H, psi)`, `twoLevelEnergies(x)` ([lower, upper] of [[x, -1], [-1, -x]]) and `twoLevelWeak(x)` (1 + x^2/2); `interferometerPort(phi, +-1)` (|(I +- R_z(phi))|+x>/2|^2, repeats at 4 pi); Rabi: `rabiProbability(w0, w1, w, t)` (closed form, Townsend Eq. 4.45), `rabiPeak(delta, w1 = 1)`, `rabiFlipTime(w1)` (2 pi/w1) and `rabiExactProbability(w0, w1, w, t, steps)` (RK4 of Eq. 4.38 as written, to measure the rotating-wave error). `constants.ts` `M_MUON`, `larmorG(f, B, m)` (4 pi m f/(e B)), `photonFrequency(lambda)`, `photonEnergy(lambda)`.

**Townsend pass, G4b (448 L13; numpy `pipeline/claims_448/g4b.py` -> `__fixtures__/claims-448/g4b.json`, 32 keys, and `__fixtures__/scatter-g4b.json` engine cases; tests `physics/scatterG4b.test.ts`):** `wave.ts` box (infinite walls at `lo` and `lo + L`, hbar = m = 1): `boxEnergy(L, n)`, `boxPairEnergy`, `boxMomentumSpread`, `boxState(g, L, n | [n1, n2], t, lo)` (each level turns by its own phase), `boxNodes`, `boxOverlap(a, b)` (closed form for two eigenstates {lo, hi, n}); `scatter.ts`: `stepRT(E, V0)` (R and T as current ratios, Eq. 6.135; R = 1, T = 0 below the step), `barrierT(E, V0, a, m, hbar)` (Eq. 6.144; E = V0 and E above V0 handled), `barrierThick`, `decayConstant`, `probabilityCurrent`, `totalCurrent`, `continuityResidual`; `constants.ts` `boxEnergyEv(L, m, n)`.
