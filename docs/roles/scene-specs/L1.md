## 0. Rules every beat follows

**One shot + one change per beat.** The change happens in the beat window (±0.35 beat, smoothstep); sweeps and
counts run over the hold (`f.hold`, linear). Reduced motion: every change is a cut, sweeps take 3 stops, the
reader clock stands still (no atom flow), the unit-entry reveal is complete.

**Scenes draw `f.state`, never decide physics.** Atom fates and plate spots are seeded uniform draws against the
engine's exact fractions (`theory.blocked[k]`, `plus`, `minus`) — like `fireMany`. Numbers on screen: θ (a state
input), Born % and |α|²/|β|² (engine), the drawn sample's counts, and ⟨σₙ⟩ = (plus − minus)/(plus + minus).

**Shot library** (`scenes/lab/shots.ts`, lens = 36 mm-wide equivalents, horizontal fov via `hfovToVfov`):

A focal change of more than two ladder rows (28 → 35 → 40 → 50 → 65 → 85) is a **cut** at t = ½.

**Cut rule (D §4.0 "a reconfiguration that is not the beat's change happens under a cut").** A bench topology
change is a cut when the bench count or a source changes, modules drop off the END, or the topology changes
together with the model or the field. A module added in place (l1-quantized:b4, l1-sequential:b3) slides in
from −x and a middle module removed (l1-sequential:b6 reveal) slides out, both inside the window.

**Deposit runs.** A plate grows 0 → 1400 over the hold of the first beat of a run (same topology + model, deposit
not cleared, no tilt sweep); later beats of the run show it full. A sweeping beat shows a rolling 300.
`batches` shows N per batch. `deposit: 'clear'` shows none. Nothing lands before the unit-entry beam front
reaches the plate (front: u 0 → 0.5 on the unit's first beat). An old plate slides +x 2.6 u and stays at 35 %.

**Spoiler rule.** A lab beat followed by a `model: 'classical'` beat stops the beam at the last magnet's exit,
so the plate is never seen before the classical question is asked (l1-quantized:b1 → b2).

**Readouts** (top-right column): `+ n · − m` (sample) and `x % + · Born y %` (engine) per bench — not in split
panes, not for the uniform field or the classical overlay; `θ = …°` on tilt beats; `P(+) = … · 2P(+) − 1 = …`
(`fill-bar`) or `⟨σₙ⟩ = …` (`centroid`); plane `|α|² = …`, `|β|² = …` with the shadows.

**Labels** go through the D layout pass (`scenes/labels.ts`): reserved zones = passports, readout column,
caption, inset (+ 8 px) and the lab gizmo; candidates = anchor, then ±22/±38 px; a moved label gets a 1 px
silver leader; no spot → it fades out. Callouts (OVEN, MAGNET, PLATE), state chips (with a leader to their beam),
gradient-arrow names (z / x / n̂), spot labels (±ħ/2, ±1 in the black box, fractions with `fractions`), stop
tags (`block · ½` with `blocked`), badges (dashed silver), orientation gizmo (lab only, bottom-right, above a
wide caption). Term focus (`f.focus`) outlines the linked label and brightens the linked 3D object.

**Near-camera ghosting.** A magnet the camera sits in or brushes (≤ 0.8 u) fades to 8 %; in end-on shots the
C-yoke is ghosted to 28 % so the pole pair + gradient arrow read as a clock hand.
