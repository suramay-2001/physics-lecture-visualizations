## 2. O1 — Slowing a sodium beam with one laser (Introduction + Ch. 1)

**Apparatus.** An oven at 712 K sends Na through a 1.1 m solenoid wound in layers of decreasing length; a laser tuned below resonance runs against the beam; a weak chopped probe laser 40 cm downstream reads the speed distribution from fluorescence. **Measure.** The speed distribution before and after: pushed down and squeezed. **Try it.** `slower-force`, `zeeman-slower`.
- `o1-why` §Intro, pp. 6–7. Beam brightness is capped by the collimation/flux trade-off; finite flight time forces Δν·Δt ≥ 1; moving clocks run slow (second-order Doppler); Einstein expected photon recoil to be negligible, and for one photon it is, but an atom can scatter a very large number of them. *Claims:* `o1TransitHz` 167 Hz (`flightLimit(3, 500)`), `o1Doppler2Beam` −1.39×10⁻¹² (`secondOrderDoppler(500)`). Link `qc-l13-plane-wave` (a photon of wave number k carries ħk). Figure: none.
- `o1-force` §1.1–1.2, pp. 8–10. Absorb, then emit in a random direction: the average kick is ħk per absorbed photon, so the maximum deceleration is a_max = ħkγ/2M (half the time in the excited state); the Lorentzian rate (1.1), saturation intensity, and a Table 1.1 row for Na. *Claims:* `o1RecoilNa` 2.95 cm/s (`recoilVelocity`), `o1AmaxNa` 9.07×10⁵ m/s² (`maxAcceleration`), `o1IsatNa` 6.26 mW/cm² (`saturationIntensity`), `o1VPeakNa` 879 m/s (`slowRow(NA_D2, 712).vPeak`), `o1StopLenNa` 0.426 m, `o1StopTimeNa` 0.970 ms (`slowRow`), `o1HGammaOverK` 12.1 m/s (Correction C2, `gammaOverK(H_LYA)`). Figure: `plot` series `scatterVsDetuning` (s = 0.1, 1, 10). Links `qc-l12-driven`, `qc-l11-two-level`.
- `o1-compensation` §1.2.1, pp. 10–13. After a few γ/k of slowing the Doppler shift walks the atom out of resonance; three cures: sweep the laser at k·a (chirp), tune the atom with a field B(z) = B₀√(1 − z/z₀) so that ħk v_R(z) = μ′B(z), or use white light (≈ k v₀/γ times more power). Rydberg Stark slowing: one sentence. *Claims:* `o1DopplerOverGamma` 152 (`dopplerOverGamma`), `o1ChirpGHzMs` 0.769 GHz/ms (`chirpRate`) (opt), `o1SlowerB0G` 1,066 G (`zeemanSlower(NA_D2, 879, 0.5).B0`), `o1SlowerLenM` 0.852 m (`.z0`), figure: `plot` curve `slowerField` (x = z/z₀, y = B/B₀ = v_R/v₀; marker at z/z₀ = ½ → 0.707). **Correction C1** (p. 11). Link `qc-l1-quantized` (energy of a moment in a field).
- `o1-probe` §1.2.2, p. 13. The apparatus above; the probe's speed resolution is γ/k; result in words. No claim. Link `qc-q8-mixed` (a speed distribution is an ensemble).
- `o1-cooling` §1.3–1.3.2, pp. 13–15. Slowing is not cooling: temperature is the width, and atoms below v₀ pile up near one final speed; damping time M/β = 2/γ_R at η = ½, s = 2; the beam's angle grows as 1/v and recoil random-walks the atoms sideways. *Claims:* `o1DampUs` 12.7 µs (`slowerDamping`), `o1Divergence` 0.2 rad (`divergence(0.01, 1000, 50)`) (opt), `o1TransDiffMm` 6.58 mm (`transverseDiffusion`). Link `qc-q8-mixed`.

## 3. O2 — Optical molasses (Ch. 2)

**Apparatus.** Two opposed red-detuned beams (retro-reflected), then three orthogonal pairs; a Rb beam crossing a transverse pair and imaged on a hot grid; Na dropped from the molasses into a probe 18 mm below. **Measure.** Force against speed; spot size; fall-time distribution → temperature. **Try it.** `molasses-force`, `doppler-limit`.
- `o2-friction` §2.1–2.2, pp. 17–19. Each beam is resonant with atoms moving the right way; the sum is linear in v near zero (2.2) with friction β; capture velocity v_c = γ/2k; the book's formula for the force maxima agrees with a numerical maximum. *Claims:* `o2BetaK2` 0.444 ħk² (δ = −γ/2, s = 1; `molassesBeta`), `o2VcNa` 2.89 m/s (`captureVelocity`), `o2PeakVel` 0.598 γ/k (`molassesPeak`, numerical; the book's formula gives the same), `o2LinErrVc` 25 % and `o2LinErrHalf` 5.9 % (opt) (how linear the law is at v_c and v_c/2). Figure: `plot` series `molassesForceVsV` (beam +, beam −, sum). Link `qc-l12-driven`.
- `o2-limit` §2.2, pp. 18–19. Recoil heating against friction: k_BT = D₀/β, D₀ = (ħk)²·2Γ_sc; minimum at δ = −γ/2 gives ħγ/2, independent of λ, M and (for s ≪ 1) intensity. *Claims:* `o2TDNa` 235 µK (`dopplerTemperature`), `o2TAtGamma` 1.25 (T at δ = −γ over T_D), `o2DiffTemp` 1.005 (D₀/β over ħγ/2 at s = 0.01, `diffusionTemperature`). Figure: `plot` curve `dopplerTVsDetuning`. Link `qc-q8-mixed`.
- `o2-collimation` §2.3–2.3.2, pp. 19–21. Transverse molasses on a Rb beam: momentum width falls from ≈ 2v_c to √(ħγ/2M), a compression √(γ/γ_R) per axis; phase-space volume falls by γ/γ_R per two axes; minimal spatial spread Δx = (2v_c)²/2a_max. *Claims:* `o2RbGOverGR` 1,609, `o2RbDvCms` 11.8 cm/s, `o2RbVc` 2.37 m/s, `o2RbCompress` 40.1, `o2RbDxUm` 99.9 µm (**Correction C3**, p. 20), `o2PSCompress` 4.75×10⁶ (opt; with the oven's longitudinal compression), `o2SpotArea` 23.0 (opt; (6 mm/1.25 mm)²). Figure: none. Link `qc-l13-plane-wave`.
- `o2-dressed` §2.3.3, pp. 21–23. Strong light: dressed pair split to ħΩ, Ω = √(γ²s/2 + δ²), light shift ħ(Ω − |δ|)/2, mixing cos 2φ = −δ/Ω; atoms climb more hills than they descend when δ > 0 ("blue cooling"), force not capped by ħkγ/2. *Claims:* `o2DressedOmega` 1.414 γ, `o2DressedShift` 0.207 ħγ (opt) (s = 2, δ = +γ; `dressed`). Figure: `plot` curve `dressedOmega`. Links `qc-l4-eigen`, `qc-l11-stationary`.
- `o2-3d` §2.4, pp. 23–24. Three pairs make a viscous region, not a trap: atoms random-walk out; the ballistic temperature came out far below T_D (words); unbalanced beams still hold atoms by a drift speed. *Claims:* `o2WalkStepNa` 2.62 µm, `o2WalkStepsNa` 1.45×10⁷ (steps to diffuse 1 cm), `o2WalkTimeNa` 92 s (`molassesWalk`). The book gives about thirty seconds: ours is about three times longer (note N2, not a Correction). Link `qc-q8-mixed`.

## 4. O3 — Below the Doppler limit (Ch. 3)

**Apparatus.** A pair of counter-propagating beams with orthogonal polarizations (lin⊥lin, or σ⁺ and σ⁻) on Rb or Cs atoms with several ground sublevels; coils for a weak or strong uniform B; hot-wire or time-of-flight readout. **Measure.** Velocity peaks (at 0 or ±v_r) and temperature against light shift. Word budget is tightest here: 6 sections × 2 paragraphs ≤ 75 words. **Try it.** `sisyphus-hills`, `vsr-force`.
- `o3-gradient` §3.1–3.2.2, pp. 27–30. Non-adiabatic following of optical pumping needs sublevels; in lin⊥lin (Jg = ½ → Je = 3/2) the light is σ⁺, linear, σ⁻, linear over λ/2; the shift (3.1) of m = +½ is 3× that of m = −½ in σ⁺ light; an atom climbs λ/4 hills and is pumped at the top, losing the hill height; friction β ≈ ħk²|δ|/γ, larger than the Doppler maximum by 2|δ|/γ. *Claims:* `o3CGUp` 1, `o3CGDown` 0.333 (`clebschSquared`), `o3U1` −17.9 µK (Rb, δ = −4γ, s = ½, `lightShiftGround`, C² = 1), `o3HillDepth` 11.9 µK (`sisyphusHill`; model: §13 item 3), `o3FrictionRatio` 8. Figure: `plot` series `sisyphusPotential` (m = ±½). Links `qc-l4-eigen`, `qc-q8-pure-rho`, `qc-q8-mixed` (populations of sublevels).
- `o3-milc` §3.3, pp. 30–31. A standing wave of fixed σ⁺ plus a weak transverse B: Larmor precession at the nodes, pumping at the antinodes; works for ω_Z < kv < γ_p; 1D Doppler speed v_D. *Claims:* `o3MilcLow` 3.64 cm/s (ω_Z/k at 0.1 G, 85Rb F = 3), `o3VDRb` 9.87 cm/s (**Correction C4**, `dopplerSpeed1D`), `o3VDSimple` 11.8 cm/s (opt). Link `qc-l11-two-level`.
- `o3-sigma` §3.4, pp. 31–33. σ⁺σ⁻: linear light whose axis turns once per λ; no hills, only lag; Jg = 1 → 2, m = +1 scatters σ⁺ six times as well as σ⁻. *Claims:* `o3CGOne` 1, `o3CGSix` 6 (`clebschSquared`). No figure. Link `qc-q8-mixed`.
- `o3-vsr` §3.5–3.6, pp. 33–35. Strong field: ground states split by ħω_Z; Raman resonance when the Doppler shifts of the two beams match, cooling to v_r = ω_Z/2k (MILC, lin⊥lin) or ω_Z/k (σ⁺σ⁻); force (3.3) is a dispersion curve at v_r. *Claims:* `o3ZeemanMHzG` 0.467 MHz/G (`zeemanOmega`), `o3VsrSigma` 36.4 cm/s and `o3VsrLin` 18.2 cm/s at 1 G (`vsrVelocity`; **Correction C5**), `o3Eq33Peak` 0.5 (opt). Figure: `plot` series `vsrForce` (σ⁺σ⁻ and lin⊥lin at 0.5 G). Links `qc-l12-driven`, `qc-l11-two-level`.
- `o3-theory` §3.7–3.8, pp. 35–40. Operator theory: F = Tr(σ_gg F_eff) splits into four parts (radiation pressure, dipole, two polarization-gradient forces) with the common prefactor of the Doppler force, so sub-Doppler friction is steeper, not stronger; Sisyphus is one idea behind four cases (Rydberg, dressed, lin⊥lin, MILC): dissipative through spontaneous emission, irreversible because the emitted phase is lost. No claim, no figure. Links `qc-q8-trace-rule`, `qc-q8-mixed`.
- `o3-3d` §3.9, pp. 40–42. In 3D the limit scales with the light shift, k_BT = c ΔE_g (c = 0.125 lin⊥lin, 0.097 σ⁺σ⁻), floor at the recoil temperature; Cs data on one line against light shift (words). *Claims:* `o3TlimLin` 2.24 µK and `o3TlimSigma` 1.74 µK (Rb, same δ, s as above; `subDopplerLimit`), `o3CsTD` 125.6 µK, `o3CsTR` 198 nK, `o3CsTDoverTR` 633 (`dopplerTemperature`, `recoilTemperature`; **Correction C6**). Link `qc-l1-quantized`.

## 5. O4 — A magnetic bowl for atoms (Ch. 4)

**Apparatus.** Two coils with opposite currents (spacing 1.25 R) give B = 0 at the centre; Na from a Zeeman slower; an on-axis probe records absorption against B, an rf field flips sublevels. **Measure.** Trapping time (words), the absorption spectrum that maps the atoms' positions. **Try it.** `quadrupole-orbit`, `airy-levels`.
- `o4-why` §4.1, pp. 46–47. Earnshaw forbids a field maximum, so a moment seeks a field minimum; the energy is stored inside the atom, so levels shift and traps are shallow (≪ 1 K) and need cold atoms and vacuum. *Claims:* `o4GaussUK` 67.2 µK/G (`zeemanEnergyK`), `o4DepthmK` 13.4 mK (200 G wall; `trapDepthK`), `o4Vesc` 3.12 m/s (opt). Link `qc-l1-quantized`.
- `o4-quadrupole` §4.2, pp. 47–48. F = −∇(μ·B), |B| = A√(ρ² + 4z²), potential rises linearly, twice as steeply along z; neither central nor harmonic, so angular momentum is not conserved; the moment must follow the field. Figure: `plot` series `quadrupoleU` (radial, axial). Links `qc-l1-quantized`, `qc-l4-average`.
- `o4-orbits` §4.3–4.3.2, pp. 48–49. Circular orbit: v = √(ρa), T = 2π√(ρ/a), a = μA/M; adiabaticity ω_Z ≫ ω_T gives ρ ≫ (ħ²/M²a)^{1/3}, v ≫ (ħa/M)^{1/3}; the non-adiabatic region is a negligible fraction of the volume; orbits chaotic near the depth (words). *Claims:* `o4TrapAccel` 243 m/s², `o4OrbitV` 1.56 m/s and `o4OrbitT` 40.3 ms (ρ = 1 cm; `circularOrbit`), `o4RhoMin` 0.316 µm, `o4VMin` 0.876 cm/s (`adiabaticLimits`), `o4OrbitN` 5.64×10⁶ (= ω_Z/ω_T), `o4LarmorMHz` 140 (opt), `o4NonAdiabVol` 3.9×10⁻¹⁵ (opt). See note N1. Link `qc-l11-two-level`.
- `o4-quantum` §4.4–4.4.3, pp. 50–53. Heuristic quantization L = nħ gives E_n = (3/2)E₁n^{2/3}, E₁ = (Ma²ħ²)^{1/3}; in 1D the V-shaped well has Airy levels (zeros of Ai′ then Ai alternate); bound states mix with the continuum so none is strictly bound; sub-recoil cooling is needed to see the levels. *Claims:* `o4E1kHz` 4.42 (`heuristicE1`), `o4AiryE1kHz` 3.57, `o4AiryE2kHz` 8.20, `o4AiryE3kHz` 11.4 (`vTrapLevels`; Na, A = 1 T/m), `o4AiryRatio21` 2.295 vs `o4HeurRatio21` 1.587 (the n^{2/3} law is asymptotic). Figure: `plot` series `airyLevels` + `heuristicLevels` (x = n). Links `qc-l13-stationary`, `qc-l13-flow`.
- `o4-experiments` §4.4.4, pp. 53–54. First trap limited by vacuum, a superconducting Ioffe trap held atoms for minutes, absorption and rf spectra reveal where the atoms sit, laser and rf cooling of trapped atoms (all words). No claim. Link `qc-l12-driven` (the rf flip is a driven two-level atom).

## 6. O5 — Traps made of light (Ch. 5)

## 7. O6 — What cold atoms are for (Ch. 6, Ch. 7 Summary)

- `o6-beams` §6.1, pp. 65–67. Brighter beams from transverse cooling: deflection, an "atomic funnel", converging beams and multiply reflected light, a magneto-optic lens (words; results not digits). Link `qc-l1-quantized`.
- `o6-clocks` §6.2, pp. 67–69. The second is 9,192,631,770 cycles of the Cs hyperfine line; transit time and second-order Doppler limit a thermal beam; a fountain gives T between two passes and a Ramsey fringe of width 1/2T. *Claims:* `o6FountainT` 0.350 s (15 cm above the cavity; `fountainTime`), `o6RamseyFountain` 1.43 Hz (`ramseyWidth`), `o6BeamT` 6 ms and `o6RamseyBeam` 83.3 Hz (3 m at 500 m/s), `o6Doppler2Fountain` −1.65×10⁻¹⁷ (v = 1.72 m/s), `o1Doppler2Beam` (cited), `o6PrecRatio` 1.63×10⁻¹³ (**Correction C8**). ## 8. Errata (verified on the page image; each has an engine check; "our calculation finds …")

- **C1 p. 11.** B(z) = B₀√(1 − z₀/z) and v_R = v₀√(1 − z₀/z) are printed with z₀ and z swapped (imaginary for z < z₀), and z₀ ≡ mv₀/(ηħkγ) is a time.
