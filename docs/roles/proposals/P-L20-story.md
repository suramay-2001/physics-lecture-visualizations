## 6. Review cards
- **reverse**: scattering fixes E and solves r, τ; a bound state is normalisable and E is found · eq `\int|\varphi|^2dx<\infty` · trap: "any k fits inside, so any E".
- **infinite**: φ(0) = φ(a) = 0 gives k_n = nπ/a, E_n = n²π²ħ²/2ma², n half-waves, n − 1 nodes, φ_n = √(2/a) sin(nπx/a) · eq `E_n = \tfrac{n^2\pi^2\hbar^2}{2ma^2}` · trap: imposing φ′ continuous at an infinite wall.
- **finite**: φ even or odd; k tan(ka/2) = κ or −k cot(ka/2) = κ with k² + κ² = 2mV₀/ħ² · eq `k\tan\tfrac{ka}{2}=\kappa` · trap: reading a tail as particles leaving.
- **compare**: finite levels lie below the infinite ones, finitely many; two levels make the density move at (E₂ − E₁)/ħ · eq `|\Psi|^2 = \tfrac12(\varphi_1^2+\varphi_2^2+2\varphi_1\varphi_2\cos\tfrac{(E_2-E_1)t}{\hbar})` · trap: calling a bound state motionless.
- **oscillator**: V ≈ ½mω²x², H = (ħω/2)(X² + P²), a†a = ½(X² + P² − 1) · eq `H = \hbar\omega(\hat a^\dagger\hat a + \tfrac12)` · trap: dropping the commutator.
