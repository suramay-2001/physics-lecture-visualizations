Step 3 of 9

The plan has two gates: a Hadamard on the top qubit, then a CNOT that lets the top qubit steer the bottom one.

|Φ+⟩ = CNOT01 (H ⊗ 𝟙) |00⟩. We apply the two factors in order and track the state after each.

Step 4 of 9 · The Hadamard on the top qubit

The Hadamard gate acts on the top qubit. It turns a sure |0⟩ into an even mix of |0⟩ and |1⟩.

DerivationH|0⟩ = |+⟩

  1. Write the starting state as a column of two numbers. The top slot counts 0, the bottom slot counts 1, and |0⟩ is all 0.

    |0⟩ = 10

  2. The Hadamard gate is a 2 × 2 table of numbers with 1/√2 out front.

    H = 1√2111−1

  3. To apply a table to a column, take each row, multiply it slot by slot with the column, and add.

    top: 1·1 + 1·0 = 1    bottom: 1·1 + (−1)·0 = 1

  4. Put the 1/√2 back in front of both answers.

    H|0⟩ = 1√211 = 1√21√2

  5. Read the column back as kets: equal parts |0⟩ and |1⟩. This state has a name, |+⟩.

    H|0⟩ = |0⟩ + |1⟩√2 = |+⟩

  6. Check: square each amplitude and add. The chances must total 1.

    (1√2)² + (1√2)² = ½ + ½ = 1

Seen |+⟩ before? It is Spin Lab's |+x⟩: send it through a z-magnet and the atoms split half and half.

The Hadamard H = (X + Z)/√2 is Hermitian and unitary, so H² = 𝟙. It maps the computational basis to the X eigenbasis: H|0⟩ = |+⟩, H|1⟩ = |−⟩.

DerivationH|0⟩ = |+⟩

  1. H|0⟩ = 1√2111−110 = 1√211

  2. = (|0⟩ + |1⟩)/√2 ≡ |+⟩,   ⟨+|+⟩ = 1

With |0⟩ ≡ |+z⟩ (shared with Spin Lab), |+⟩ ≡ |+x⟩, a member of the three mutually unbiased bases.

Step 5 of 9

The bottom qubit is still |0⟩. Joining the two gives one column of four numbers, one for each pair: 00, 01, 10, 11.

(H ⊗ 𝟙)|00⟩ = |+⟩ ⊗ |0⟩ = (|00⟩ + |10⟩)/√2, a product state: it factors, so it is not entangled yet.

Lecture 2 · Unit 2.5

Three bases, each blind to the others

You came here from Physics 709, Q8, step 4. There this state is written |+⟩ = H|0⟩; here it is |+x⟩. Same state, same colours: |0⟩ ≡ |+z⟩ is amber in both courses, |1⟩ ≡ |−z⟩ is cobalt.

Beat 3 of 7

The state |+x⟩ = (|+z⟩ + |−z⟩)/√2 sits on the equator of the Bloch sphere. Send it into a z-magnet and half the atoms land up, half land down.

That is what "blind" means here: a z-measurement learns nothing about x.

Step 4 · The Hadamard on the top qubit

The Hadamard H = (X + Z)/√2 is Hermitian and unitary, so H² = 𝟙. It maps the computational basis to the X eigenbasis, and under |0⟩ ≡ |+z⟩ the image |+⟩ is Spin Lab's |+x⟩.1

H|0⟩ = 1√2111−110 = (|0⟩ + |1⟩)/√2 ≡ |+⟩

Step 5 · Adding the second qubit

The bottom qubit is untouched, so the joint state is the Kronecker product of the factors, ordered |00⟩, |01⟩, |10⟩, |11⟩ with qubit 0 leftmost:

|+⟩ ⊗ |0⟩ = 1√211 ⊗ 10 = 1√21010 = (|00⟩ + |10⟩)/√2

It factors, so it is a product state; the CNOT in step 6 is what entangles it.
